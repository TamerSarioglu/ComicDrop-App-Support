Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, comic files, reading history, or local files from your device. All your comic files (CBR, CBZ, etc.) remain strictly on your local device or your personal iCloud storage.

However, the Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you for advertising purposes. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the Application. AdMob may use advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA) and collect usage data to serve personalized ads based on your interests.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS/iPadOS)

On Apple devices, the Application requests your permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (using the App Tracking Transparency framework) to deliver personalized advertisements. If you ask the app not to track, you will still see advertisements, but they may be less relevant to you. You can manage this preference at any time in your device privacy settings.
